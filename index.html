About Me

Rooted in the Himalayas. Inspired by the World.

I come from Chamoli, Uttarakhand, a region known for its magnificent mountains, cultural richness and deep connection with nature.


While engineering defines my professional journey, creativity, filmmaking, cultural heritage, social responsibility and exploration represent another important part of who I am.


This website is a space to document the experiences, memories and milestones that have shaped my life beyond my profession.
